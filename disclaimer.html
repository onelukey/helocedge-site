Financial Disclaimer

Last Updated: June 2026

Not Financial Advice

The information provided on HELOC Edge (helocedge.com) and through our interactive tools, including the HELOC Calculator, is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute formal financial, legal, tax, or real estate advice.

Home equity products, including Lines of Credit (HELOCs), are complex financial vehicles that carry significant risks, up to and including the potential loss of your home through foreclosure if payments are not maintained. Interest rates are highly variable and subject to change based on market conditions.

Accuracy of Calculations

While we strive to ensure that the mathematical formulas utilized in our online tools are accurate and up-to-date, all calculated projections are estimates only. Your actual loan terms, monthly payments, closing costs, and interest rates will differ depending entirely on your chosen financial institution, credit score, debt-to-income ratio, and appraisal valuation.

Seek Professional Assistance

We strongly encourage you to consult with a certified financial planner, licensed mortgage broker, or qualified banking professional prior to making any borrowing decisions or signing any binding financial contracts. Never rely solely on an online calculator or article to execute real-world financial actions.
