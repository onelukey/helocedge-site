What Builds Your Home Equity?

Home equity can change through two main factors: mortgage paydown (principal payments reduce your loan balance) and changes in home value (which can increase or decrease). Early in many amortizing mortgages, a larger share of each payment goes toward interest. The actual mix depends on the loan terms and payment amount.

Why Equity Matters for a HELOC

Your equity position directly determines how much you can borrow via a HELOC. Some lenders use combined loan-to-value limits around 80–85%, but limits and underwriting requirements vary. The higher your equity, the larger the credit line available to you. Use the Borrowing Power Calculator to explore an illustrative credit-line estimate.

Appreciation is not guaranteed

This tool projects equity using a consistent appreciation rate. In reality, home values fluctuate and can decline. The default 3% is an example, not a forecast. Try 0% or a negative rate to explore other scenarios.
