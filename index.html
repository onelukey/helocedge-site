How Does a HELOC Work?

A HELOC functions like a revolving credit card backed by the equity in your home. Unlike a standard home equity loan, you don't receive a lump sum all at once. Instead, you are approved for a specific credit limit and can draw funds as needed during the **Draw Period**.

The Two Critical Phases of a HELOC

  • The Draw Period: Usually lasting 10 years, you can withdraw money whenever you like. Most lenders only require you to make interest-only payments during this time, keeping your monthly expenses exceptionally low.
  • The Repayment Period: Typically lasting 15 to 20 years, your line of credit is locked—meaning you can no longer withdraw money. You must now pay back both the principal amount borrowed and the interest, which will cause your monthly payments to rise significantly.
